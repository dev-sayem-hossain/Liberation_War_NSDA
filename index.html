Liberation War Archive

Preserving the documents, voices and photographs of the 1971 Liberation War of Bangladesh.

About the Archive

The Liberation War of 1971 was the nine-month struggle in which the people of Bangladesh won independence from Pakistan. It ended with victory on 16 December 1971 and gave birth to the nation of Bangladesh.

This archive collects important documents, recorded interviews and photographs so that the sacrifices of the freedom fighters and the people can be remembered and studied by future generations.
